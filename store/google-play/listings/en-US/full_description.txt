ThinkOfMe is the easiest way to know what the people you love really want, and to keep the surprise.

MAKE A WISH LIST IN SECONDS
• Paste a link from any store: the name, photo, price and store fill themselves in.
• Snap a photo of something you love, in a shop, a magazine or at a friend's place, and ThinkOfMe reads the product, brand and price for you.
• Add details like size or color, and mark how much you'd love it.

SHARE IT WITH YOUR PEOPLE
• Create a circle for your partner, your family or your friends, and invite them with a link.
• Choose which circles see each wish.

KEEP THE SURPRISE
• When someone is getting you a gift, they mark it as theirs. Everyone else in the circle sees it's taken, so no one buys the same thing, and you never find out.
• Plan it, then mark it as bought.

NEVER MISS A DATE
• Save birthdays and anniversaries. Get a gentle reminder to update your list before your day, and a nudge to find a gift before theirs.
• Holiday reminders for Valentine's Day, Tu B'Av, Mother's and Father's Day, Hanukkah, Christmas, Eid and more. You choose which ones.

MADE FOR EVERYONE YOU LOVE
• In English, Hebrew, Arabic and Russian, with full right-to-left support.
• Free, with small ads that never cover the screen.

Your lists are visible only to the circles you choose. You can delete your account and all your data at any time in Settings.
