Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

ThinkOfMe lets you keep a wishlist, share it with people you choose, and quietly coordinate gifts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address, provided by Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a relay address instead.
  • Profile: the name you choose and optional "About me" details such as sizes, favorite colors, allergies and things you'd rather not receive.
  • Content you add: wishes (titles, links, photos, prices, notes), dates such as birthdays and anniversaries, the circles you create or join, and which wishes you plan to buy for others.
  • Device data: a push notification token, your time zone and language, so reminders arrive at a sensible local time in your language.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic usage events, to find and fix problems.
  • Advertising: if ads are shown, our ad provider may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location, subject to your consent (see below).

Who can see what

  • Your wishes, dates and "About me" details are visible only to members of the circles you share them with.
  • When someone plans to buy one of your wishes, that information is visible to other members who can see the wish, and never to you. This is enforced by our servers, not only by the app.
  • When you add a link, our server visits that page to read the product's title, photos and price, and keeps a copy of the product photos with your wish. If the page doesn't state them clearly, its text is sent to our AI provider to pick them out.
  • When you add a photo to a wish, it is sent to our AI provider to read the product's name, store and price so we can fill in the details for you. The result goes back to your app only.

How we use it

To run the app: sync your lists across your devices and your circles, send reminders you can turn off in Settings, prevent abuse, fix problems, and show ads that keep the app free. We don't sell your personal information.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (data storage, sign-in, notifications, crash reports). Data is stored in the European Union (Belgium, europe-west1).
  • Google AdMob (ads). See how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Apple and Google for sign-in and for delivering push notifications.
  • Anthropic (AI that reads wish photos and product pages to suggest product details). Photos are processed to answer the request and are not used to train AI models.

Ads, tracking and consent

In the EEA, the UK and some US states we ask for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone we ask for permission before using your advertising identifier. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account any time in Settings → Delete account; this permanently deletes your profile, wishes, dates, photos, and your membership in circles. Deletion from our database is immediate; residual copies in server logs are removed within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict some processing. Most of this is available in the app; for anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

ThinkOfMe is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by server-side security rules that are automatically tested.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and tell you in the app.
