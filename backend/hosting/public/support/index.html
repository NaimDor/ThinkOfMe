Common questions

Will the person I'm buying for see that I claimed their wish?
No. Claims are hidden from the wish's owner by our servers, not just by the app.

How do I join my partner's or family's circle?
Open their invite link, or go to Circles → Join with a code and enter the 8-character code.

How do I stop reminders?
Settings → Reminders, or turn off individual holidays in Settings → Holidays.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your data.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required by law).

Delete your ThinkOfMe account

In the app (immediate): open ThinkOfMe → Settings → Delete account, and confirm.

Without the app: email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__ from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my ThinkOfMe account". We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted: your profile, wishes, photos, dates, claims and circle memberships. Circles you created are deleted when you are their last member. Residual copies in server logs are removed within 30 days. Nothing is kept afterwards.
