Common questions

Will the person I'm buying for see that I claimed their wish?
No. Claims are hidden from the wish's owner by our servers, not just by the app.

How do I join my partner's or family's circle?
Open their invite link, or go to Circles → Join with a code and enter the 8-character code.

How do I stop reminders?
Settings → Reminders, or turn off individual holidays in Settings → Holidays.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your data.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required by law).
